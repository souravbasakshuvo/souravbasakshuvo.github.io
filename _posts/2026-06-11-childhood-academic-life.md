My educational journey has been shaped by two institutions that left deep impressions on who I am today — [Jamalpur Zilla School (JZS)](https://jzs.edu.bd/) and [Govt. Ashek Mahmud College (AMC)](https://amc.edu.bd/), both in Jamalpur, Bangladesh. These years were not just about textbooks and exams — they were about discovering what I was capable of, learning how to compete, and finding joy in things beyond the classroom.

My academic performance was recognized through Government Board Merit-based Scholarships at two levels. In the Primary School Certificate (PSC) Examination, I was placed in the **Talent Pool** category by the Dhaka Education Board — the highest tier of the scholarship — receiving a monthly stipend of 300 BDT from January 2011 to December 2013. Later, in the Junior School Certificate (JSC) Examination, I earned the **General Grade Scholarship** from the Board of Intermediate & Secondary Education, Dhaka, with a monthly stipend of 200 BDT from January 2014 to December 2015. I went on to achieve GPA 5.00 in both SSC (2016) and HSC (2018).

But academics was only one side of school life. I participated in **Science and Mathematics Olympiads**, which pushed me to think beyond rote learning. In 2017, I ranked **11th in the Mymensingh Regional Contest** of the National High School Programming Contest (NHSPC) — my earliest foray into competitive programming. That same year, I was crowned **Regional Champion** in The HSBC Prothom Alo Language Competition, organized by HSBC and Prothom Alo Bangladesh — a competition I was proud to represent and win.

Sports were equally close to my heart. I was the **Badminton Champion** of my class as early as Class 3 — perhaps the beginning of a lifelong love for the sport. During my time at JZS, I also finished as **Runner-up in Chess** at the Intramural Sports Competition 2015, which made my later BME Chess Championship win feel like a full-circle moment.

Away from academics and sports, I had a quieter passion: music. I performed and won awards in **Rabindra Sangeet, Nazrul Sangeet**, and **Deshattobodok Gan** — genres rooted in the cultural and patriotic soul of Bengal. These experiences taught me that a full life is built from many different kinds of learning.

Looking back, the years at JZS and AMC gave me more than grades — they gave me curiosity, resilience, and a sense of who I wanted to become.
